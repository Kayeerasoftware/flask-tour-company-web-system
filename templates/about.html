Our Story

Who We Are

DEHAPIZ Tour and Travel Uganda is a proudly Ugandan-owned and operated tour company headquartered in Kampala, Uganda. Founded with a deep passion for showcasing the extraordinary natural beauty, rich wildlife, and vibrant cultures of the Pearl of Africa, DEHAPIZ has grown into one of Uganda's most trusted names in tourism.

Our name, DEHAPIZ, represents the spirit of discovery — the belief that every journey should be transformative, every destination should leave a lasting impression, and every traveller should return home with stories worth telling. We specialise in crafting personalised safari experiences, gorilla trekking expeditions, cultural tours, adventure activities, and corporate travel packages across Uganda.

We are fully licensed and registered with the Uganda Tourism Board (UTB) and are proud members of the Association of Uganda Tour Operators (AUTO). Our commitment to responsible, sustainable, and community-based tourism ensures that every trip you take with us directly benefits the local communities and conservation efforts in Uganda.

🎯
Our Mission

To provide exceptional, authentic, and affordable Ugandan travel experiences that connect people with nature, wildlife, and culture — while uplifting local communities and promoting conservation.

🌟
Our Vision

To be East Africa's leading tour company, recognised for excellence in service, deep local expertise, and a genuine commitment to sustainable tourism that benefits Uganda and its people.

💚
Our Values

Integrity, authenticity, sustainability, community empowerment, and a relentless passion for delivering unforgettable experiences. We treat every traveller like family.
